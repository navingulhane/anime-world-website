How Anime is Made

Anime is created through a step-by-step process that begins with Manga. Manga are Japanese comic books that provide the story, characters, and artwork for anime productions.

Steps in Anime Creation

Step No. Description
1Manga is created by a mangaka (artist).
2Popular manga is selected for animation.
3Storyboards and scripts are prepared.
4Characters and scenes are animated.
5Voice acting, music, and sound effects are added.
6Final editing is done before release.
