How Anime is Made

Anime is created through a step-by-step process that begins with Manga. Manga are Japanese comic books that provide the story, characters, and artwork for anime productions.

Steps in Anime Creation

Step No. Description
1 Manga is created by a mangaka (artist).
2 Popular manga is selected for animation.
3 Storyboards and scripts are prepared.
4 Characters and scenes are animated.
5 Voice acting, music, and sound effects are added.
6 Final editing is done before release.
